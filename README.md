# Throw It!
Throw it is a small indie game centered around the art of... playing in the snow!

**DEFEND THE CRYSTAL!**

Stranded in uncharted territory after crashing, you must defend the only thing that can save and protect the innocent habitants of this world. Protect a powerful but fragile crystal from evil robot snowmen sent from afar, bent on killing you and retrieving their precious crystal.

**ENGINEER YOUR VICTORY!**

Develop technologies to fight for you using scraps from robots! As the robots evolve wave after wave, you must use what they've left behind to fight back.

**BUILD A FORT!**

Build the highest turrets and the toughest strongholds to ensure your safety, or just use your imagination and play in the sandbox!
